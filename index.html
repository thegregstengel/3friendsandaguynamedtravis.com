Okay, why does this exist?

Our friend Travis started a podcast. A whole podcast. And then he didn't invite his three closest friends onto it.

So we did the only mature, emotionally healthy thing available to us: we built an entire movie website and a competing podcast of the same name, purely out of love. And spite. Mostly spite.

This is that website. The movies are real. The feelings are real. The grudge is petty eternal.
